Reading: T = 6688 us per block at 2x. A step of g blocks costs g*T + n*R, with n the integer (positive or negative) that gives the smallest value above a minimum L,
and R the time of one rotation of the disc at that place: from the steps 2 and 4,
  R(5000) = 68.7 ms, R(35000) = 79.6 ms, R(65000) = 89.1 ms; R^2 grows linearly with the block number (constant linear velocity): R^2 = 4452 + 0.0536 * block, in ms^2 (this CD-R).
  The minimum L is below 33.5 ms for steps of 5 blocks and about 37 to 45 ms when the head must change track (17 blocks at 35000: 34 ms is refused, 65 blocks: 36.7 ms is refused).
