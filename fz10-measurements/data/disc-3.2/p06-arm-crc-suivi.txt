P2I BENCH 3.2 ARM CRC SUIVI (ARM CRC tracking) - FZ-10 (photo 06-arm-crc-suivi.jpg). Flags NZCV after muls r4,r5,r6 with r6 = 1 << k, k = 0..31 (one hexadecimal digit per k).

FA 0AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA   flags in 0, r5 = 7FFFFFFF
FB 3BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB   flags in C V, r5 = 7FFFFFFF
FC 80000000000000000000000000000008   flags in 0, r5 = 80000001
FD AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA   flags in C, r5 = FFFFFFFF
C0 E7EBC6D4 003C0168                   families MUL and MULS
CAT 14 RUN 0                           stops there (see p05-arm-pas-a-pas.txt)

Opera: FA 08888888..., FB FC FD the same as the console; MUL E7EBC6D4, MULS 5F6D097E.

Reading: the four rows fit "C is the carry out of the barrel shifter at the last step of the Booth multiplier (shift 0 keeps C)", but that rule does not give the CRC of the family MULS (52497F13 against 003C0168), nor do 32 variants of it (scratchpad mes/arm_muls.py, arm_muls2.py): the rule for general multipliers is not found.
The order of evaluation of the bench's generator is left to right (the model gives the CRC of the family MUL, and Opera's for MULS when C is kept).
