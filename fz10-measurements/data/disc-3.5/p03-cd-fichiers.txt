Reading:
- sequential reads match (L1 SUITE median 6688 = one block at double speed, core 6656; 512 KB in 1.80 s, core 1.77-1.78 s, 1.5-2 %
  fast);
- the first file open after the long pause of the photos takes 1.33 s on the FZ-10, 0.11 s on the core: the drive (or the driver)
  needs about 1.2 s to come back after an idle period; the core has no such state (to measure: idle time before it happens);
- a block read one field after the previous one (from the drive's buffer) takes 4.6-4.9 ms on the FZ-10, 5.7 ms on the core;
- jumps of 2000 blocks spread over 87-205 ms on the FZ-10 (median 146 ms: seek plus the wait for the disc to come round), while
  the core gives a constant 125 ms (its seek table has no turn of the disc beyond 256 blocks).
