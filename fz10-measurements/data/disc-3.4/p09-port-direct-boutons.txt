P2I BENCH 3.4 PORT MANETTE DIRECT (direct control port) - FZ-10, 2026-11-11 (photos 09a-09d)
Live control port buffer (200 bytes from IN = 001FD584, refreshed every 8 fields), chain console - gun 2 - pad, on the page itself
(text on a dark screen). Words 0-41 are zero in every photo, word 43 = FFFFFFFF, words 46-49 = FFFFFFFF. Word 44 = gun bytes 0-3,
word 45 = gun byte 4, then the pad (80 00), then FF.

 photo   state                               word 44     word 45     gun byte 1
 09a     nothing pressed                     4D000000    008000FF    00
 09b     trigger held                        4D800000    008000FF    80
 09c     calibration button held             4D480000    008000FF    48
 09d     trigger and calibration button      4DC80000    008000FF    C8
(byte 1 of 09c and 09d checked on enlarged crops)

Reading:
- Gun byte 1 holds the buttons: trigger = 80, calibration button = 48 (two bits, 40 and 08, every time), both = C8.
- The calibration button is an ordinary button bit read by the software, like the trigger; nothing in the counter data moves when it
  is pressed, which fits the remainders of p08 (same time base before and after the press).
- Counter and line count are 0 in all four photos: the gun sees nothing on this dark page.

- The gun measures at every field whether or not the trigger is pressed: the trigger is only bit 80 of byte 1. With the thin white

- The counters are even here too.
- Pad bytes 80 00 with no pad button pressed; PAD (DoControlPad) 00000000.
