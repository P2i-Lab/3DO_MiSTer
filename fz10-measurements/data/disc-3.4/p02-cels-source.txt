Reading:
- The steps of the time per row stay at the same widths whatever the alignment of the source (offsets 0-3 words): the fetch groups start at the row,
  not at a 16-byte boundary. Offsets change the times by 8 % at most (W14, W18 at offset 0).
- A source in VRAM costs the same as in DRAM (with ACE 0-4 % faster).
- With a blend the drawing is slower than the reading, yet steps remain (W10-12 +2144, W16-18 +1968, W24-26 +2000 against about +1300 per 2 pixels):
  the fetches are not hidden behind the slower drawing.
