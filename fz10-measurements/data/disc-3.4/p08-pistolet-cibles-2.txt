The gun counter runs at 20 MHz, in step with the picture (36 samples: p06 guns A and B, p07 gun 1, this page):
- Taken modulo one line, the counters group by the column of the cross. The period that groups them best is 1271.09 counts per
  line (searched 1200.00-1350.00 in steps of 0.01); 20 MHz x 63.556 us = 1271.11. Remainder per column, in counts:
     x =  40:  gun 1  267  283  265   gun 2  259  282  269   2-gun A  260  290  268   2-gun B  199  258  265   (careful mean  271)
     x = 160:  gun 1  664  652  655   gun 2  649  676  656   2-gun A  619  650  682   2-gun B  596  608  599   (careful mean  659)
     x = 280:  gun 1 1047 1012 1058   gun 2 1049 1005 1029   2-gun A 1035 1029 1078   2-gun B 1023 1030  991   (careful mean 1033)
  So counter = 1271.1 x line + about 144 + 3.18 x column. A 320-pixel line at 6.136 MHz would give 3.26 per pixel: the difference
  is within the aim scatter (5 to 8 pixels).
- At the same cross, gun 2 minus gun 1: +2534, -3828, +2544, +12710, +8922, +6348, +2546, +12712, +3784 = 2, -3, 2, 10, 7, 5, 2, 10, 3
  lines of 1271, each within 30 counts.
- Both guns have the same clock and the same place in the line. The slopes found before (1187, 1192, 1225, 1303 per line) came from
  the vertical aim, which is the weaker one: the line index wanders by up to about 9 lines around its mean.
- Line index (counter div 1271.09) minus the line of the cross: gun 1 18 17 14 / 2 7 8 / 3 3 9; gun 2 20 14 16 / 12 14 13 / 5 13 12.
  If the gun latches on the first line it sees, about hits / 2 above its aim, the counter's line 0 is about 22-24 lines before
  picture line 0, so near line counter 255 of the previous field (picture line 0 = counter 16 on the VCNT page). Rough: it rests on
  the aim and on hits / 2.
- The remainders do not depend on the run: the calibration button the tester pressed before the two-gun run left the time base and
  its place in the line unchanged. To see what that button sends: PORT MANETTE DIRECT (direct control port) (live buffer), word 44 = 4D 80 b2 b3.
- Gun 2 sees 20-23 lines, gun 1 25-29. The counter is even in all 36 samples.
