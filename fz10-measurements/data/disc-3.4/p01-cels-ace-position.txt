Reading:
- The parity of the first line changes nothing in 16 bits (ACE 11120-11296) and adds 2 % in 6 bits (6768 even, 6928 odd): the two corner engines share the
  write cycles whether or not their pixels fall in the same 32-bit word of the frame buffer.
- Rows shifted from each other by up to 1 pixel per row cost the same as unshifted rows, with and without ACE; at 2 pixels per row the cel costs 26 %
  more without ACE (16 bits) and 83 % more (6 bits), and ACE still gains 39 % (16 bits) and 50 % (6 bits).
- Mirrored cels cost 1-5 % more than plain ones.
- Half blend: ACE gains 40 % (16 bits) and 43-44 % (6 bits).
