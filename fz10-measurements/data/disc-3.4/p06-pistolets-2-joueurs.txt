Reading:
- Each gun answers with 5 bytes: 0x4D, 0x80 (both guns, trigger held), then a 20-bit counter and a 5-bit count. Opera's layout fits:
  the counter grows with the row, the count is 22-31 lines on a white screen (the lines the gun sees).
- In the window: zeros up to 167, FF FF FF FF at 168-171, gun 1 at 172-176, gun 2 at 177-181, the pad (80 00) at 182-183, then FF.
- Counter per line, from the row means (first line seen = line of the cross - hits / 2): gun A 1193 (18.8 MHz), gun B 1303
  (20.5 MHz); line 0 of the picture is at 15.5 and 8.3 lines of counter. The two guns differ by 9 %: aim or gun clocks.
- In every row the counter falls from left to right (row 120: -25954 for A, -34818 for B), while the beam takes only about
  +1000 counts to cross the row: the aim varied by up to about 25 lines, so these points give the vertical scale only.
- Gun A sees 27-31 lines, gun B 22-26.
- Update (p08): both guns count at 20 MHz, 1271.1 per line, in step with the picture; the slopes above come from the aim.
