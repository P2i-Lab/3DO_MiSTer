Reading:
- A cel of one row takes the same time with and without ACE (4.4 / 4.5 us at W = 2, 9.75 / 9.9 us at W = 32): one row is one engine.
- Time of a cel: about 3 us + H x the time per row of the width (page 1/3).
- Rows contiguous in memory: 1 to 6 % saved at W = 8, 16, 24, 32 (a whole number of groups of four words), 9 to 27 % at W = 4, 12, 20, 28: when a row ends inside a group of four words, the rest of the group serves the next row only if the rows are contiguous.
