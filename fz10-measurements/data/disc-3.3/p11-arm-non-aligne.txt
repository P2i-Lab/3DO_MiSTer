Reading:
- Every word access at an address that is not word aligned stops the task on the FZ-10: load, store, block transfer, swap, in DRAM and in VRAM (MADAM abort cause ABT_BWACC, "byte address, word access"; the kernel kills only the task at fault: the page went on without a restart).
- Byte accesses at any address work, on the big-endian lanes: LDRB +1 reads 12, STRB +1 writes byte 1 (11 A4 13 14).
- The stops of the families LDR on discs 3.0 to 3.2 are explained. Opera and the core do not raise this abort.
