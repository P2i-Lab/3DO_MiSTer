Reading (ns per source pixel, 76800 pixels):
- All opaque / all transparent: 6 bits no ACE 141 / 87, ACE 91 / 43; 16 bits no ACE 212 / 118, ACE 148 / 71.
- 16 bits without ACE: the time is the sum of 212 ns per opaque and 118 ns per transparent pixel within 4 %, whatever the layout (half transparent: 13040 12992 13008 12832 against 12672 for the mean).
- 6 bits without ACE: half transparent 1:1 to 4:4 is about the mean (+2 %), blocks of 8 or more are 9 to 11 % below it, and one opaque in four is as cheap as all transparent (6704 against 6672).
- With ACE, whole rows alternately opaque and transparent cost much more than the mean: 6576 against 5160 (6 bits), 12384 against 8384 (16 bits): the two engines work on two rows at once and lose the gain.
- The core spends 45 ns on a transparent 6-bit pixel without ACE (disc 3.0, TRANSP100 3472 against 6512 on the console).
