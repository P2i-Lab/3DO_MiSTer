Rule found (model: scratchpad mes/arm_muls3.py):
C after MULS or MLAS is the carry out of the barrel shifter at the last step of the multiplier, 2 bits per step, step n looks at bits 2n+1:2n of Rs and a carry c:
  c = 0:  00 nothing                c stays 0
          01 add Rm << 2n           c stays 0
          10 sub Rm << (2n+1)       c becomes 1
          11 sub Rm << 2n           c becomes 1
  c = 1:  00 add Rm << 2n           c becomes 0
          01 add Rm << (2n+1)       c becomes 0
          10 sub Rm << 2n           c stays 1
          11 nothing                c stays 1
The multiplier stops after step n when the remaining bits of Rs are zero and c = 0, or after step 15. C = bit (32 - shift) of Rm for the shift of that last step, also when the step does nothing; with shift 0, C is unchanged.
Rn and Rd = Rs change nothing.
Checked: the 36 rows of pages 1 and 2, the 24 random words of page 3 (all identical), and the CRC of the family MULS of the bench: 003C0168 = FZ-10 (disc 3.2). MUL E7EBC6D4 also equal.
Mistake of the earlier attempts (arm_muls.py): they took c = 1 after t = 2 whatever the incoming carry; with c = 1 and bits 01 the step adds 2 x Rm and c becomes 0.
