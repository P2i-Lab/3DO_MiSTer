Reading:
- 8 bits coded without ACE: one more word of source (4 pixels) costs 0.33 us per row (the same 0.083 us per pixel as in 16 bits), except from word 3 to 4 and 4 to 5, 7 to 8 and 8 to 9, and so on every 4 words: 0.7 to 1.0 us there, so two steps of 0.4 to 0.7 us every four words.
- 6 bits coded: the same two steps per four words (3-4, 4-5, 7-8, 8-9, 11-12).
- 16 bits (page 1/3) has the same two steps every four words, one word later: 4-5 and 5-6, 8-9 and 9-10, 12-13 and 13-14.
- The width of the steps cannot be placed better than one word (4 pixels) at 8 bits with this list of widths.
