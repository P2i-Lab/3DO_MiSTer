Reading:
- ACE saves 19 to 31 % when the vertical scale is at most 1, and only 2 to 6 % as soon as it is above 1 (1.1 to 1.7). It comes back partly at 2 (17 %), 2.5 (17 %) and 3 (34 %).
- The break depends on the vertical scale alone: Y only with ACE goes from 4832 at scale 1 to 9168 at 1.41 (x 1.9), X only with ACE goes smoothly from 4832 to 5680.
- X 2 Y .5 with ACE is 5312 against 7216 without (26 % saved): a horizontal scale above 1 does not stop the two engines.
- Without ACE the time grows regularly: X only 155 to 419 ns per source pixel, Y only 145 to 535 ns.
- 8 bits coded against 16 bits: 1760, 1680, 1824 us less for 32768 source pixels at scale 0.5, 1, 2, about 52 ns per source pixel, whatever the scale.
- Half blend adds 150 to 173 ns per screen pixel without ACE, 124 to 158 ns with ACE.
